Match-Fit Maintenance

How To Wash & Care For Your Kits

Football kits are high-performance synthetic garments. Follow these 6 golden rules to keep your badges, heat-pressed names, and fabric sheen looking brand new for years.

🔄

Always Wash Inside Out

Turn your jersey inside out before dropping it in the washing machine. This protects the printed sponsors, custom names, and crests from friction against other clothes.

❄️

Cold Water Only (Max 30°C)

Hot water breaks down the heat-activated adhesives used on rubberized badges and sleeve patches. Stick to cold water cycles to prevent peeling.

🚫

Strictly No Tumble Dry

Never put your football jerseys in a clothes dryer. Extreme machine heat will warp micro-mesh fabrics and crack vinyl prints. Hang them to air dry in the shade.

⚠️

Never Iron Over Badges

Do not apply direct heat from an iron over heat-pressed sponsors, names, or rubberized crests. If you must iron creases, do it inside out on a low setting.

🧴

Avoid Fabric Softeners

Fabric softeners strip away the sweat-wicking properties of 130gsm player-version mesh fabrics. Use a gentle liquid detergent instead.

🖐️

Hand Wash Custom Kits

For custom name & number prints or multi-patch international kits, a quick 10-minute hand wash in cold water will maximize the lifespan of your prints.

💡

A Note on Heat-Pressed Vinyl (Sponsors & Names)

Both our Player and Fan versions use match-accurate heat-pressed rubberized badges for sponsors and custom names. High heat from tumble dryers or washing machines will dissolve the vinyl adhesive over time. Treat them gently, air dry in the shade, and they will survive hundreds of matchdays.
